## Key Takeaway

When building side projects or designing products, the hardest part isn't writing code — it's not knowing what the interface should look like. Staring at a blank canvas in Figma? Instead of imagining from scratch, just look at how real products do it.

---

## 1. Mobbin

**The world's largest mobile + web design pattern library**
🔗 mobbin.com

**Four browsing dimensions:**
- By **App Category** (AI, Health, Food & Drink, etc.)
- By **Screen Pattern** (Dashboard, Filter & Sort, Notifications, etc.)
- By **UI Element** (Text Field, Bottom Sheet, Tab Bar, etc.)
- By **User Flow** (Onboarding, Sign In, Payment, etc.)

Covers both iOS and Web platforms. The Web section additionally supports filtering by style (Dark, Minimal, Colorful, Motion, etc.).

**Best for**: When designing a feature and wondering "how do others do this" — e.g., "what does a typical subscription page look like" or "how to design an onboarding flow."

**Free vs Paid**: Free users can browse the latest 4 Apps/websites; Pro unlocks everything.

---

## 2. UI Notes

**Real product UI screenshot library for Chinese apps — 396 apps, 150K+ screenshots**
🔗 uinotes.com

396 apps, 152,592 screenshots — only "shipped designs," no mockups or concept work. Featured apps include: Doubao, DeepSeek, Coze, Hongguo Short Drama, Yuanbao, TapTap… Filterable by company (ByteDance, Tencent, Alibaba, etc.) and industry. Each app shows its version number and last update time.

**Best for**: Building products for the Chinese market — checking competitor UI screenshots and design trends.

**Free vs Paid**: Basic browsing is free; membership unlocks more features.

---

## 3. Checklist Design

**56 design best-practice checklists**
🔗 checklist.design

Instead of showing you others' designs, it tells you **what to watch out for when designing something**. 56 checklists across five categories:

- **Website (13)**: Login page, Billing, Blog Post, Shopping Cart, FAQ, etc.
- **Components (24)**: Button, Modal, Input Field, Toast, Carousel, etc.
- **Flows (13)**: Add to Cart, Cancel Subscription, Reset Password, Delete Account, etc.
- **Topics (3)**: Responsive Design, UX Copy, Dark Mode
- **Brand (3)**: Logo, Typography, Tone of Voice

Has a **Figma plugin** to check items directly inside your design tool.

**Best for**: Self-reviewing after finishing a design, or establishing standards when starting a new component. Great for beginners building design system awareness.

**Free vs Paid**: Completely free.

---

## 4. Page Flows

**Real product user flow recordings and screenshots**
🔗 pageflows.com

The core feature is screen recordings — you can watch the complete process from opening an app to completing a specific action, with step-by-step annotations. For example, "How does Airbnb's search flow guide users?" or "How does Dropbox's onboarding work?"

Covers iOS, Android, Web, and Emails — 100K+ designers use it. Filterable by category, industry, and UX pattern.

**Best for**: Designing user flows (onboarding, sign-up, payment, etc.) and researching how top products handle them.

**Free vs Paid**: 3-day free trial, then $8.25/month (annual) or $13/month (quarterly).

---

## 5. Refero

**Design research platform built for humans and AI**
🔗 refero.design

The most granular classification — six dimensions fully covered:
- **Page Types (45)**: Dashboard, Landing, Paywall, 404, Blog, Profile, etc.
- **Flows (44)**: Sign Up, Login, Subscribe, Add to Cart, Chat, Edit, Delete Account, etc.
- **UX Patterns (87)**: Testimonials, Filter & Sorting, Dark Mode, Shopping, etc.
- **UI Elements (69)**: Modal, Cards, Table, Tabs, Skeleton, Chart, etc.
- **Sites (by Industry)**: AI Tool, SaaS, Finance, Education, Food, etc.
- **Fonts**: Filter product pages by typeface used

Features benchmark products like Stripe, Calendly, n8n, Shopify, Coinbase. Supports **MCP integration**, allowing AI agents to access the design library directly for AI-powered site building with real product references.

**Best for**: Web product design research, especially SaaS products.

**Free vs Paid**: 7-day free trial, then paid subscription.

---

## 6. Lovable Discover

**AI-built app showcase platform**
🔗 lovable.dev

Lovable is an AI website builder, and its Discover page is an excellent source of design inspiration. All apps displayed here were built through AI conversation, giving you a tangible sense of "what AI site building can achieve today." The range spans CRM, event marketing, music players, icon libraries, and more.

Each app has collect/bookmark data showing community approval. Browse by Featured, Builders, Personal, Marketing, Company, and more.

**Best for**: Seeing real AI-built websites in action, or finding rapid prototyping inspiration. Also great as demo material when convincing your team to try AI-powered building.

**Free vs Paid**: Browsing is free. Lovable itself offers free credits for building; advanced features are paid.
